About this trek

Started at Lapsi and going CW, so that by sunset the most technically difficult West coast is done. Sunset in Mellieha, leaving only Selmun area as a bit technical for darkness. The night then progresses on the East coast which is mostly flat and paved roads. Sunrise was enjoyed in Valletta, and then the rest of the day till the eve to route back to Lapsi along the South coast.
